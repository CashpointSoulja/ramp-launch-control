# Jobs to be done

## Product Operations Specialist (primary)

**When** feedback arrives from tickets, calls, NPS and shared Slack channels across four product areas, **I want to** have it sorted into themes and sent to the right owner with a reason I can defend, **so I can** spend my time on the ambiguous items and on sizing what matters, not on copy-pasting.

**When** a launch date is close, **I want to** see in one place whether docs, support, quality and sign-offs are actually done, with evidence, **so I can** call go or no-go without chasing four people on Slack.

**When** something breaks, **I want to** track severity, SLA and customer impact, then drive a review to owned action items, **so I can** make sure it doesn't happen twice.

## Area product manager

**When** I plan the next quarter, **I want to** see which themes affect the most distinct accounts and the most ARR, **so I can** prioritise on evidence rather than whoever shouted loudest.

## Support lead

**When** a launch is coming, **I want to** know my team's macros and training are a gate, not an afterthought, **so I can** avoid surprise ticket spikes.

## Engineering lead

**When** an incident is resolved, **I want to** a short, structured review with clear owners, **so I can** fix the cause instead of writing a long document nobody reads.

## Hiring manager reading this

**When** I evaluate a Product Operations candidate, **I want to** see how they think about feedback, launches and incidents as one system, **so I can** judge whether they would raise the bar on day one.
